# research console checkpoint

The console now follows one research loop: notice a difference in the public subset, pin companies for side-by-side review, then inspect the dated evidence. Overview, compare, and explore replace the prior six equal-weight views. Market-wide activity, coverage limits, and pinned source inventories remain available as contextual disclosures.

The numerical layer stays descriptive. Revenue, growth, assets, and net income margin come from the existing selected SEC cells. Growth compares adjacent selected periods for one company. Margin requires aligned revenue and net income periods. Charts keep a visible zero baseline and break at missing periods. Fiscal period dates, filing dates, accessions, source dates, and cohort limits remain visible; no investment scores, predictive rankings, recommendations, or market-size estimates were added.

The stable checkpoint consists of the export-backed console, pure tested research model, shared UI primitives, additive warehouse observation views, and explicit architecture contract. Advanced analytics can build on this only after a new source and selection policy earn them.
